Poorani Ravindhiran

Roboticist, Autonomous Vehicle Team, Volkswagen

Email: poorani@umich.edu

I’m a robotics software engineer with a passion for developing AI and perception applications for robots. I’m actively doing my “bit” to building a future where AI enhances the way we live and work.

I hold a Master’s degree in Robotics from the University of Michigan, where I built a strong foundation in classical computer vision, deep learning, robotic systems, and the essential math fundamentals. Currently, I’m working on sensor assessment and perception pipelines for an exciting project at Volkswagen: the vintage-inspired, fully electric, autonomous ID.Buzz.

Prior to this, I worked at Qualcomm, where I developed video validation algorithms and scaled testing through automation using deep learning techniques for tasks such as video artifact classification, frame order detection, and audio-video synchronization. I not only worked on training and deploying models but also optimized them for real-time performance. I’ve also had the opportunity to intern on a perception project involving a warehouse robot. Outside of work, I love cooking, painting, playing board games, and exploring new cultures.

Feel free to reach out if you’d like to discuss any cool projects or chat!
